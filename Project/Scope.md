\Project Scope



\Project Objective



Our project objective is to provide a centralized platform for managing the university internship process. The system aims to reduce manual work and scattered communication by allowing students, internship coordinators, academic supervisors, and company supervisors to manage internship applications, placements, supervisor assignments, weekly logbooks, and student progress through one organized system.



\ Target Users



\ Students: Apply for internships, view their placement information, and submit weekly logbooks.

\- Internship Coordinators: Manage student applications and placements, assign supervisors, and monitor overall internship progress.

\- Academic Supervisors: Monitor assigned students, review weekly logbooks, provide feedback, and follow academic progress.

\- Company Supervisors: Supervise students at the workplace, review relevant progress information, and provide feedback on student performance.



\ In-Scope Features



\- User login and access for students, internship coordinators, academic supervisors, and company supervisors.

\- Student internship application submission and tracking of the application status (submitted, approved, rejected, etc.).

\- Internship placement management, managing where each student will complete the internship after the application stage, including the assigned company, internship location, department, start and end dates, and whether the placement is confirmed by the company or still pending.

\- Assignment of academic and company supervisors to students.

\- Weekly logbook submission by students.

\- Review, comments, approval, or request for revision of weekly logbooks by academic supervisors.

\- Student internship progress, including monitoring completed internship weeks and tracking the submission status of weekly logbooks.

\- Basic notifications or reminders for important actions and missing submissions.

\- Post-internship feedback management, allowing students to evaluate their internship experience and company supervisors to provide feedback on the student's performance after the internship is completed.



\ Out-of-Scope Features



\- Video calling or online meeting functionality which could allow students, internship coordinators, academic supervisors, and company supervisors to hold meetings directly through the system. It could support progress discussions and follow-up meetings without relying on external platforms.

\- A mobile application which could give users easier access from smartphones. It could make it more convenient to submit logbooks, review updates, receive notifications, and check internship information while on the go.

\- Automatic AI-based matching of students with companies where students would upload their CVs to the system, and the AI would analyze information such as their skills, qualifications, experience, and areas of interest. It would then compare this information with available internship opportunities and company requirements to recommend the most suitable companies for each student.

\- Automated official completion certificate generated after a student successfully completes the internship containing the student's name, company, internship period, and completion status.



\ Major Deliverables



\- Project planning and requirements documentation which includes the project scope, selected process model, risks, stakeholder analysis, and feasibility study.

\- System models and design documentation which includes appropriate UML diagrams and database design.

\- User interface prototype showing the main screens and workflow of the proposed system.

\- Functional web-based system implementing the main in-scope features which includes internship applications, placement management, supervisor assignment, weekly logbooks, progress tracking, notifications, and post-internship feedback.

\- Database for storing users, internship applications, placements, supervisor assignments, logbooks, progress information, and feedback.

\- Source code and version history maintained in the GitHub repository.

\- Testing documentation and test results to verify that the implemented features work correctly.

\- Basic user documentation explaining how each type of user performs the main tasks in the system.
