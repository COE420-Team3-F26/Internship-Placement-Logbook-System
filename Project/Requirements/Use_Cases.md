**Use Cases**



* Miriam Almimi Contributions



UC-01: Login to System - Users authenticate themselves and access the functions available according to their role.

UC-02: Submit Internship Application - Student completes and submits an internship application with required information and documents.

UC-03: Review Internship Application - Coordinator reviews submitted applications and approves or rejects them. 

UC-04: View Weekly Logbooks - Academic supervisor views submitted weekly logbooks from assigned students.

UC-05: Submit Company Feedback - Company supervisor evaluates student performance after internship completion.




* Raghad Diab Contributions


UC-06: Submit Weekly Logbook - Student creates and submits weekly internship progress records.

UC-07: Review and Approve Logbook - Academic supervisor reviews logbooks, adds comments, and approves or requests revision. 

UC-08: Receive Notifications - Users receive reminders about pending submissions, revisions, or reviews. 

UC-09: Submit Student Feedback - Student submits feedback about the internship experience after completion.
 
UC-10: Assign Internship Supervisors - Coordinator assigns academic and company supervisors to a student placement. 




* Ahmad Amin Contributions


UC-11: Manage Internship Placement - Coordinator creates and updates internship placement information including company and internship dates.

UC-12: View Internship Status - Student views application status, placement details, and assigned supervisors.

UC-13: Upload Internship Documents - Student uploads required documents related to the internship application or placement.

UC-14: Track Internship Progress - Student, internship coordinator, and academic supervisor monitor completed internship weeks and logbook submission status.

UC-15: Filter Student Internship Records - Coordinator filters students based on application or placement status.

UC-16: Request Logbook Revision - The academic supervisor requests a revision when a submitted weekly logbook contains issues or requires corrections.




* Miriam Almimi and Raghad Diab Contribution


UC-17: Upload Additional Documents - The student uploads additional documents when they are requested or required for a specific internship application. 



* Use Case Relationships


R-01: Submit Internship Application <<include>> Upload Internship Documents - When a student submits an application, uploading required documents is always part of completing the application process. 

R-02: Review and Approve Logbook <<include>> View Weekly Logbooks - Reviewing and approving a logbook requires the academic supervisor to first view the submitted logbook before evaluating it and providing approval or revision comments. 

R-03: Manage Internship Placement <<include>> Assign Internship Supervisors - Creating a complete placement record requires assigning the responsible supervisors.

R-04: Review and Approve Logbook <<extend>> Request Logbook Revision - Revision is optional and occurs only when the submitted logbook contains issues or requires corrections. 

R-05: Submit Internship Application <<extend>> Upload Additional Documents- Additional documents may be required only for certain internship applications, so this behavior extends the normal application process.
