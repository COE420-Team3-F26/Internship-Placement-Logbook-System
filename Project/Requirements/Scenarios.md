**Scenarios**



S-01 – Student Submits Internship Application



Actor/Stakeholder: Student



A student logs into the system, completes the internship application form, uploads the required internship documents, and submits the application. The system records the application and allows the student to track its status.



S-02 – Internship Coordinator Reviews Applications



Actor/Stakeholder: Internship Coordinator



The internship coordinator accesses submitted applications, reviews student information and documents, and updates the application status as approved or rejected. The student is then able to view the updated status.



S-03 – Student Placement and Supervisor Assignment



Actor/Stakeholder: Internship Coordinator, Student, Academic Supervisor, Company Supervisor



After an application is approved, the internship coordinator creates the student's placement record, assigns the company and academic supervisors, and updates the system with internship details such as company, position, and internship dates. The student can view the confirmed placement information.



S-04 – Weekly Logbook Submission and Review



Actor/Stakeholder: Student, Academic Supervisor



During the internship period, the student submits weekly logbook entries describing completed activities. The academic supervisor reviews the submitted logbook, provides comments, and either approves it or requests revision.



S-05 – Internship Completion Feedback



Actor/Stakeholder: Student, Company Supervisor



After completing the internship, the student submits feedback about their internship experience, while the company supervisor submits an evaluation of the student's performance. The system stores both feedback records for future reference.
