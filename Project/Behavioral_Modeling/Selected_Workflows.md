# Selected Workflows

For Lab 7, we used the same three use cases selected in Lab 6 to develop the activity diagrams.

## UC-02: Submit Internship Application

The student starts an internship application, enters the required information, and uploads the necessary documents. The system checks the documents and application completeness before submission.

## UC-07: Review and Approve Logbook

The academic supervisor reviews a submitted weekly logbook and decides whether to approve it or request a revision. If a revision is required, the logbook status is updated and the student is notified.

## UC-11: Manage Internship Placement

The internship coordinator creates a placement for an approved application, retrieves company details, and assigns academic and company supervisors. The supervisor roles are checked before the placement information is updated.
