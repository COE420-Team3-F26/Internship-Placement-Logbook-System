# Project Stakeholder Analysis

The system involves stakeholders who use it directly, oversee the internship process, or contribute to the project's development and success.

---

## Students

**Role:**  
Students apply for internships, view placement details, track their progress, submit weekly logbooks, and provide feedback after the internship.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
Students expect a system that is easy to use and clearly presents application statuses, placement details, deadlines, and logbook progress.

**Influence on the System:**  
Their requirements and feedback on usability help shape the interface, workflows, and core system features.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The system gives students a single place to manage internship activities instead of relying on scattered emails, documents, and messages.

---

## Internship Coordinators

**Role:**  
Internship coordinators handle applications and placements, assign supervisors, and monitor internship progress overall.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
They expect a system that organizes internship information, reduces manual work, and provides a clear view of each student's status and progress.

**Influence on the System:**  
They have strong influence because they manage the internship process and help define the workflow and several system requirements.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The system reduces manual administration and provides a clearer view of the overall internship program.

---

## Academic Supervisors

**Role:**  
Academic supervisors monitor the students assigned to them, review weekly logbooks, leave comments, approve work, or request revisions.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
They expect quick and easy access to their assigned students, submissions, progress information, and tools for reviewing student work.

**Influence on the System:**  
Their requirements influence logbook reviews, feedback, approvals, revision requests, and progress tracking.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The system allows academic supervisors to monitor multiple students and manage weekly submissions and feedback in one place.

---

## Company Supervisors

**Role:**  
Company supervisors supervise students in the workplace and provide feedback or evaluations regarding student performance.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
They expect a straightforward way to view relevant student information and submit structured feedback.

**Influence on the System:**  
Their requirements influence the post-internship feedback feature and the information related to company placements.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The system provides a structured way to report student performance instead of depending entirely on communication outside the system.

---

## University / Internship Program Administration

**Role:**  
The university or internship program administration oversees the internship program and sets the policies and requirements that students and supervisors must follow.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
They expect a properly managed and documented internship process that remains consistent with university requirements.

**Influence on the System:**  
They have high influence because university policies determine many of the system's requirements and workflows.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The system makes the internship program more consistent and organized and improves monitoring.

---

## Development Team

**Role:**  
The development team gathers requirements, designs, implements, integrates, tests, and documents the system, while also maintaining the system and its repository.

**Interests / Expectations:**  
The team requires clear requirements, appropriate tools, coordinated responsibilities, and sufficient time to deliver the required features.

**Influence on the System:**  
The development team has high influence over the system architecture, database design, usability, implementation decisions, testing, and overall system quality.

**How the System Affects Them:**  
The project determines the team's workload and requires coordination in development, version control, integration, and testing.
